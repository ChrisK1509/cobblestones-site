Who We Are

Who are the Widows Sons?

A Masonic motorcycle-riding association open to all Freemasons who enjoy motorcycling. We spread the joy of Freemasonry and raise money for charity.

Who are the Cobble Stones?

The Widows Sons Chapter for the Masonic Province of Northamptonshire & Huntingdonshire — formed of full Widows Sons and Clicker associates.

Social & Rides

An official ride once a month, plus ad-hoc rides and monthly socials at pubs across Northamptonshire & Huntingdonshire.
